How it works

Three steps. That's all.

Learn With keeps reporting frictionless so tutors spend their energy on students — not paperwork.

01
Click "Submit report"

Hit the button above. A Google Form opens in your browser — no account needed, works on any phone.

02
Fill in the session details

Your name, the student, the date, what you covered, and how the session went. About 60 seconds total.

03
Submit and you're done

Your report goes straight to Open Letter's records. Nothing else to install or set up on your end.
