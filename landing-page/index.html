The Problem We Solve

Why parents leave
traditional tutoring behind

We started Open Letter because we were tired of watching parents pay premium prices for tutoring that felt like a black box. No updates. No structure. No real connection. We built the system we wished existed for our own families.

01

Chaos

No lesson plans. No session records. No way to know if your child actually learned anything last Tuesday. Parents are left in the dark while tutors improvise week to week.

Our fix: Every session follows a structured lesson plan, logged in real time, with daily summaries sent directly to you.
02

Silence

You pay for tutoring, then hear nothing for weeks. Was the session productive? Is your child improving? Are they even attending? The silence breeds anxiety and wasted investment.

Our fix: Daily session summaries within 24 hours. Weekly wrap-ups. Weak Links analysis so you know exactly what's next.
03

Inconsistency

Tutors cancel last-minute. Teaching quality swings wildly. Your child builds rapport, then the tutor leaves. The cycle of starting over destroys momentum and confidence.

Our fix: Vetted tutors, structured onboarding, and if the match isn't right after two sessions, we re-match at no cost.
Your Journey

How enrollment
actually works

Our process is designed to be frictionless for parents and transformative for students. Every step is intentional, documented, and optimized for online delivery.

Step 01

Discovery Consultation

Complete a detailed online assessment about your child's academic standing, learning style, and goals. Followed by a brief confirmation call from our Client Experience team.

Takes 15 minutes. No obligation. We listen before we recommend.

Step 02

Tutor Matching

Our Head of Academics personally reviews your intake and selects from our vetted tutor pool based on subject expertise, teaching style compatibility, and personality fit.

Not automated. Human-reviewed. Every time.

Step 03

First Session

Your child meets their tutor in a structured virtual classroom with interactive HTML learning materials. The tutor assesses baseline knowledge and establishes rapport.

All materials are archived for future reference and parent review.

Step 04

Daily Tracking Begins

After every session, your tutor submits a detailed log covering lesson objectives, behavioral observations, and targeted areas for improvement. You receive this within 24 hours.

Starter tier: session notes via Facebook page. Essential & Premium: full email summaries + portal access.

Step 05

Continuous Feedback Loop

Weekly wrap-ups identify patterns. Weak Links targeting adjusts upcoming sessions. Parent consultations (Essential & Premium) ensure alignment between home and tutoring goals.

Premium clients receive a custom learning roadmap and bi-weekly strategy calls.
